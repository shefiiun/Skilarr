How hiring works

Four steps from "I need someone" to "job done". No running around, no begging for refunds.

Describe the job

Tell us what you need, where, and when. Add photos so the artisan sees the problem before arriving.

Compare quotes

Verified artisans near you send a price. Check their experience, past jobs and areas they cover.

Pay into escrow

Pay by card, transfer or USSD. Skilarr holds the money; the artisan sees it is secured.

Confirm and release

When the job is done to your satisfaction, confirm it and the artisan is paid the same day.

Built for trust

Meet the founder

“Nigeria is full of brilliant hands. The tailor who can copy any style from one photo, the mechanic who fixes what the dealership couldn't. What they lack is a fair way to be found and paid. Skilarr is that way.”

Shefiiun Igbaroola
Founder & CEO, Skilarr

An electronics and computer engineer and software developer, Shefiiun Igbaroola started Skilarr after seeing how hard it is for families to find artisans they can trust, and how much good work goes unseen because the artisan has no shop on a main road. His goal is simple: every skilled worker in Africa should be one search away from their next customer.
